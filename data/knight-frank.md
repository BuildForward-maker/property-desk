# Round A data — verified, Knight Frank India Real Estate H1 2026

Source: Knight Frank Research, *India Real Estate: Office and Residential Market,
January–June 2026*. Free public PDF. Captured 23 October 2026.

Everything below is from that single report unless marked otherwise. It is
transaction-tracked data from a research house, not portal asking prices — a
materially stronger source than what we currently hold.

## 1. Demand and liquidity — by zone

Knight Frank's micro-market health table for Bengaluru, H1 2026.

| Zone | Unsold inventory | YoY change | Quarters to sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| Central | 187 | −18% | 12.5 |
| East | 22,878 | +19% | 4.9 |
| North | 15,408 | +34% | 3.9 |
| South | 33,534 | +20% | 7.4 |
| West | 2,291 | +8% | 3.2 |
| **City** | **74,299** | **+22%** | **5.3** |

Age of unsold inventory, citywide: **14.4 quarters**.

**How to read QTS:** quarters needed to clear existing unsold stock at the
trailing eight-quarter sales pace. Lower is healthier. West (3.2) and North
(3.9) are absorbing fastest; South (7.4) is slowest among the volume zones.

**Central's 12.5 is a small-numbers artefact** — only 187 units. Flag it, do
not treat it as a market signal.

## 4. Infrastructure pipeline — funded and dated

### Metro, confirmed
- **Blue Line Phase 2B** — Hebbal, Yelahanka, Kempegowda International
  Airport. Under construction, **target June 2027**. This is the single most
  material near-term infrastructure event in the city.

### Suburban rail (BSTP), K-RIDE — with commissioning dates
| Corridor | Stretch | Length | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | Chikkabanavara–Yeshwanthpur–Bennigenahalli | 24.9 km | **Dec 2028** |
| 4 | Bennigenahalli–Rajanukunte; Heelalige–Bennigenahalli | 47.1 km | **Mar 2029** |
| 1 | Bengaluru city–Yelahanka–Devanahalli + Airport | 47.2 km | **Mar 2030** |
| 3 | Kengeri–Bengaluru Cantt | 16.9 km | **Mar 2030** |

Phase 1 is ~148 km over four corridors; long-term plan ~306 km. K-RIDE
estimates 1.9 mn daily journeys eventually, with a 10–20% modal shift from
road.

### Employment anchors
- **KIADB Aerospace Park, Devanahalli** — Boeing, Shell, SAP, Collins
  Aerospace. Collins' 26-acre facility takes 2,200+ employees.
- **ORR took 52.1% of all office leasing** in H1 2026, up from 24.0% a year
  earlier. PBD East another 29.3%. Together over 81% of the city.
- GCCs were 60% of office transactions, up from 53%.
- North Bengaluru office momentum building at Hebbal and the Airport Road
  belt — Amazon, BHIVE, Enzyme.

### Office rent growth by district, 12-month
CBD 17% · SBD 12.5% · PBD East 10% · ORR 6.7% · **PBD South 0% · PBD North 0%**

Office rent is a leading indicator for residential demand. PBD South
(Electronic City, Bannerghatta) and PBD North flat at 0% is worth noting
against their residential growth.

## 5. The zone mapping problem

**Knight Frank's zones are not ours.** Their classification:

| KF zone | Locations |
|---|---|
| Central | MG Road, Lavelle Road, Langford Town, Vittal Mallya Road, Richmond Road |
| East | Whitefield, Old Airport Road, Old Madras Road, KR Puram, Marathahalli |
| West | Malleswaram, Rajajinagar, Yeshwanthpur, Tumkur Road, Vijayanagar |
| North | Hebbal, Bellary Road, Hennur, Jakkur, Yelahanka, Banaswadi |
| South | Koramangala, **Sarjapur Road**, Jayanagar, JP Nagar, HSR Layout, Kanakapura Road, Bannerghatta Road |

**Sarjapur Road and HSR Layout are South for Knight Frank, East and South for
us.** Banaswadi is North for them, East for us.

**Do not re-zone our data.** Store the KF zone separately as `kfZone` and join
QTS and inventory on that. Our zones drive the UI; theirs drive the
Knight Frank join.

## 7. Proposed infra scoring rubric, 0–10

My judgement, not Knight Frank's. Worth arguing with.

| Points | Condition |
|---|---|
| 10 | Metro operational **and** a funded line or suburban corridor opening ≤3 years |
| 8 | Metro operational today |
| 6 | Funded metro or suburban rail opening ≤3 years (e.g. Blue Line areas, June 2027) |
| 4 | Funded corridor opening 3–6 years (suburban rail 2028–2030) |
| 2 | Approved but unfunded, or awaiting central clearance (Red Line / Sarjapur) |
| 0 | Nothing committed |

Add **+1** for a major employment anchor under construction within 5 km
(Aerospace Park, a new ORR campus). Cap at 10.

Under this rubric: Yelahanka and Hebbal score 10 (metro live via Green/soon
Blue, Blue Line June 2027); Devanahalli 6+1 = 7; Sarjapur Road 2;
Electronic City 8; Kengeri 8 + suburban 2030.

# ADDENDUM — H1 2025 report added

## ⚠️ I was wrong about Sarjapur Road

My earlier read — "our 14% was wrong, Knight Frank says 3%" — was mistaken.
Both are right. They describe different years.

| | H1 2025 | H1 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Sarjapur Road price growth | **16%** | **3%** |
| Price range ₹/sq ft | 5,000–11,000 | 5,700–11,300 |

Our 14% figure matches Knight Frank's H1 2025 reading of 16%. **What happened
is that growth collapsed from 16% to 3% in a single year.** Our data is not
wrong; it is a year stale.

That is a far more useful finding than a data error, and it is consistent with
everything else: South zone unsold inventory at 33,534 (+20%), the worst QTS
of any volume zone at 7.4, stalled road widening, an unfunded metro.

**The corridor has stalled. Our tool is still showing it accelerating.**
